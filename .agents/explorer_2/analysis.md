## 1. Executive Summary & Design Hybrid Strategy

The authentic redesign of SprachCafé Polnisch harmonizes two complementary visual paradigms into a unified, accessible, and culturally rich user experience:

1. **The Warm Cultural Salon Foundation (Stitch Variant 1)**:
   - Base canvas: `#FAF6EE` (warm cream linen) in light mode, `#181615` / `#24201E` in dark mode.
   - Primary brand color: `#8B1E2D` (Deep Polish Carmine / Wine Red, WCAG AA > 6:1 contrast against `#FAF6EE`).
   - Typography: Literata / Serif headings with Plus Jakarta Sans body typography.
   - Genuine community photography showcasing warm cafe encounters, language exchanges, and salon life.

2. **The Polish Poster School Accents (Polska Szkoła Plakatu — Stitch Variant 2 & Subpages)**:
   - High-contrast linocut / woodcut cultural illustrations (bold brushstrokes, expressive silhouettes, woodgrain texture).
   - Tactile vintage textures: Torn/ripped paper edges (`torn-paper`), perforated ticket stub scallops (`ticket-perforation`), typewriter date stamps, and circular rubber library seals (`stamp-verfuegbar`).
   - Folkloric and avant-garde paper cutouts (Wycinanki inspired organic shapes and bold color blocking: Slate Teal `#2B5A70`, Sage Olive `#4E653E`, Terracotta Red `#A33B32`, Honey Amber `#D97706`, Paper Cream `#FAF6EE`).
